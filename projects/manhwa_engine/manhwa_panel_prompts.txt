=== ECHO OF THE VOID — PANEL PROMPT REFERENCE v2 ===
=== KOREAN WEBTOON STYLE — COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE ===

MASTER RULE: At least 60% of panels should NOT show the MC's face.
Vary perspective: wide → detail → character → abstract → wide.

--- SHOT TYPE DEFINITIONS ---

1. ABSTRACT ESTABLISHING (new)
   Usage: World-building, cosmic imagery, first rift, system origin.
   Keywords: abstract, cosmic, energy, pattern, symbol, ripple
   Style: Pure visual composition, no geography, ethereal colors, floating elements
   Lighting: Backlit, ethereal glow, purple-violet-silver
   Example: "Massive tear in space-time, purple-black energy bleeding through, silver runes forming at the edges like stitches in reality"

2. ENVIRONMENT / LANDSCAPE
   Usage: Establish location, cityscape, void zone, atmosphere.
   Keywords: wide environmental, landscape, establishing, location
   Style: Pure location, atmospheric depth, no characters
   Lighting: Golden hour for real world, purple-violet for void
   Example: "Neo-Tokyo skyline at dusk, holographic billboards reflecting off glass towers, mag-lev trains crossing between buildings"

3. DETAIL ENVIRONMENT (new)
   Usage: Close-up of world texture, ground, wall, debris, atmosphere through objects
   Keywords: detail, ground-level, texture, close-up of environment
   Style: Sharp focus on environmental detail, shallow DOF
   Lighting: Practical light sources (neon, sun rays, void glow)
   Example: "Wet asphalt reflecting purple neon, small puddle with rainbow oil sheen, cracked pavement"

4. UI FOCUS
   Usage: System interface, notifications, holographic displays, rank screens
   Keywords: ui focus, system panel, interface, holographic, floating text, notification
   Style: Translucent dark-purple panel, silver runic borders, text materializing letter by letter
   Lighting: Self-illuminated purple-silver glow
   Example: "Void Echo System panel floating in darkness, [Host detected: Ren Kagehara] appearing letter by letter, soundwave ripples pulsing across surface"

5. OBJECT DETAIL
   Usage: Pendant, phone, weapon, item focus, emotional weight through objects
   Keywords: extreme close-up, macro, object, item, close-up of
   Style: Sharp macro focus, one object fills frame, texture emphasis
   Lighting: Dramatic highlight on object, ambient shadow
   Example: "Close-up of father's pendant, void-black stone pulsing with warm silver light from within, silver chain against torn fabric"

6. HANDS ONLY
   Usage: Show emotion through hands, avoid face, power activation aftermath
   Keywords: hands, fingers, palm, knuckles, hands-only
   Style: Only hands/arms in frame, expressive posture
   Example: "Ren's hands held in front of face, silver-violet energy fading from fingers like dying embers, cuts and scrapes visible on knuckles"

7. CHARACTER FOCUS
   Usage: Medium shot of character in environment, normal dialogue
   Keywords: medium shot, character, full body, three-quarter
   Style: Character prominent but environment visible
   Lighting: Natural scene lighting, character well-lit
   Example: "Ren weaving through traffic on beat-up electric bike, courier jacket flapping, focused expression, buildings blurring past"

8. REACTION CLOSE-UP
   Usage: Extreme emotion — fear, shock, determination. USE SPARINGLY (max 2 per chapter)
   Keywords: close-up face, reaction, emotion, shocked, terrified
   Style: Tight on face, dramatic shadows across half the face
   Example: "Ren's face frozen in terror, eyes wide reflecting purple light, sweat on brow, mouth slightly open"

9. SILHOUETTE
   Usage: Dramatic reveal, transformation, emotional beat, mystery
   Keywords: silhouette, backlit, outlined in light, shadow
   Style: Dark figure against bright light source, minimal interior detail
   Example: "Ren standing in center of fading silver-violet light, body outlined in glowing energy, shadow cast long behind him"

10. THOUGHT FOCUS (new)
    Usage: Internal monologue moment, character thinking, contemplation
    Keywords: thought, contemplative, from behind, looking, pensive
    Style: Character from behind/side, looking at something off-screen, quiet atmosphere
    Example: "Ren from behind, sitting on his futon, city lights filtering through blinds, his silhouette still against the room, system panel faintly visible in the darkness beside him"

11. ACTION / DYNAMIC
    Usage: Movement, running, fighting, dodging, mid-motion
    Keywords: action, dynamic, motion, movement, mid-action
    Style: Motion blur, dynamic angle, speed lines, energetic composition
    Example: "Ren diving sideways as Voidspawn claws tear through the space where his chest was a second ago, debris flying, action lines"

12. IMPACT FREEZE (new)
    Usage: Peak action moment, first hit, critical blow, frozen in time
    Keywords: freeze-frame, impact, frozen moment, shockwave, peak
    Style: Motion completely frozen, shockwave visible, debris suspended, cracks spreading
    Example: "Ren's fist connected with Voidspawn chest at exact moment of impact, cracks spreading like broken glass across obsidian body, ring-shaped shockwave expanding, damage numbers floating [-87 CRITICAL]"

13. POV ACTION (new)
    Usage: What MC sees while acting, first-person combat
    Keywords: pov, first-person, attacker's perspective, through eyes
    Style: No visible character body, hands/weapons only at bottom edge
    Example: "POV of Ren's fist hurtling toward camera, silver-violet energy streaming behind it like comet trail, system text '[Resonance Strike Lv.1]' flashing at bottom edge"

14. LOW ANGLE THREAT (new)
    Usage: Show power imbalance, enemy towering over, fear perspective
    Keywords: low-angle, from ground level, looking up, towering
    Style: Camera on ground looking up, subject large and imposing, skewed perspective
    Example: "Extreme low angle from ground, Voidspawn's massive silhouette blocking purple sky, four red eyes glowing down, one claw raised"

15. AFTERMATH / ATMOSPHERIC
    Usage: After battle, settling tension, quiet moment, cost of victory
    Keywords: aftermath, atmospheric, dissipating, drifting, settling, empty
    Style: Particles floating, empty space, mood lighting, stillness, debris
    Example: "Empty Void Zone alley filled with floating purple-black particles drifting down like embers, empty space where the creature fell, silence"

16. SCALE SHOT
    Usage: End of chapter, character's place in world, isolation, awe
    Keywords: extreme wide, massive scale, tiny figure, far above, dwarfed
    Style: Vast environment, character tiny or barely visible, sense of enormity
    Example: "Neo-Tokyo skyline at dusk from high above, hundreds of city lights, Ren's tiny figure barely visible walking out of alley mouth. Far above on a rooftop, a tall gaunt silhouette watches"

--- SHOT TRANSITION RULES ---

1. NEVER repeat same shot type twice in a row
2. Alternate rhythm: wide → detail → medium → abstract → wide
3. Scene opening: MUST start with environment or establishing type
4. Action sequence rhythm: action → impact → thought → action → aftermath
5. Emotional sequence rhythm: object → thought → reaction → silhouette
6. After every 3 action panels: include 1 breathing panel (environment/detail/thought)
7. Every scene needs at least 1 thought panel
8. Every major event needs 1 aftermath panel

--- COMPOSITION GUIDELINES ---

1. No MC face in >60% of panels
2. Characters in panel listed in 'characters' array — empty = AI won't insert MC
3. Vary: wide → detail → medium → abstract → wide
4. Use POV to let readers experience what the character experiences
5. Use environment shots to establish location changes
6. Use detail shots for emotional weight without showing faces
7. Every panel must advance the STORY, not just show a character
8. If speaking off-screen, note "(off-screen)" in dialogue speaker

--- SCENE STRUCTURE TEMPLATE ---

PROLOGUE Scene (~6 panels):
  P1: abstract_establishing — cosmic/first rift imagery
  P2: environment — city with rift scars
  P3: environment OR detail_environment — key world location
  P4: ui_focus OR object_detail — system/rank display
  P5: environment — human-scale perspective
  P6: scale_shot OR environment — transition to normal life

Normal Scene (~5-6 panels):
  P1: environment — establish location
  P2: thought_focus OR character_focus — see character in environment
  P3: object_detail OR detail_environment — emotional/atmospheric weight
  P4: thought OR dialogue — character thought/interaction
  P5: character_focus OR reaction — reaction or action
  P6: environment OR thought — breather

Action Scene (~5-6 panels):
  P1: environment — establish battleground
  P2: low_angle_threat — show enemy/ threat
  P3: action OR pov_action — MC moves
  P4: impact_freeze — peak moment
  P5: aftermath — settling
  P6: thought_focus — MC reaction

--- KOREAN NARRATION STYLE ---

Thought/dialogue tone guide for Ren:

- Wry, slightly cynical observations about the world
- Self-deprecating humor underneath
- Poetic but not flowery — grounded
- References to his father when things get hard
- Regret about not saying goodbye
- Determined not to be weak

Example narration tone:
"The city has a way of making you feel invisible. When you're a courier on a beat-up bike, you're just part of the scenery. Part of the noise. I was fine with that. Being invisible meant being safe."

DARK NARRATION BOX PLACEMENT:

Top of panel = scene setting / time / location
  Example: "Neo-Tokyo, 2091. Twenty years since the world learned the Void was real."

Bottom of panel = character's internal reaction
  Example: "I should have been terrified. I was. But there was something else too... something I didn't have words for yet."

Between panels = transition / thematic bridge
  Example: "Some doors, once opened, can never be closed again."
